## Keyboard shortcuts

These are Manifold's default keys. You can change most of them: see *Change a shortcut* below. To see every
shortcut as it's set on your Mac, choose **Help ▸ Keyboard Shortcuts…**.

Keys without ⌘ (such as M, I, O and N) work when the picture has the focus. While you type in a text field, they're
just letters. Some sit beside a ⌘ key on the same letter (I and ⌘I, O and ⌘O, L and ⌘L, M and ⌘M): the bare letters
follow editing apps, where I and O mark In and Out and J, K and L shuttle.

### Change a shortcut

1. Choose **Help ▸ Keyboard Shortcuts…** (also in the Manifold menu, and in Settings ▸ General).
2. Click the key beside the action.
3. Press the new key. Esc cancels; Delete removes the key.

If another action already uses that key, Manifold asks whether to move it: click **Replace** or **Cancel**. Keys that
macOS uses itself can't be chosen.

- A changed shortcut is marked with a dot. Click its arrow to put back the default.
- A category you've changed has a reset button, for example **Reset Transport**, that puts back its defaults.
  **Reset All…** puts back every default.
- **Search shortcuts** finds an action by name or key. **Find by Key** shows what a key does: click it, then press the
  key.

To use your shortcuts on another Mac, see *Settings*: Export Shortcuts and Streams….
