# Manifold 0.9.3

**Version 0.9.3 (build 26) · November 14, 2026 · Beta for testers**

## Summary

0.9.3 makes SDI and HDMI output frame-accurate and sample-accurate during file playback. With DeckLink output on, playback now follows the card's own clock, so long programs no longer repeat or skip frames on SDI, and SDI sound stays locked to its picture, sample for sample, from the first frame. SDI sound was about 2.5 frames late in 0.9.0 to 0.9.2; it is now in sync. With sound on the Mac, the Mac's sound now stays locked to the SDI picture too. Audio-only files now play to SDI and HDMI.

## Highlights

- **SDI sound is in sync.** It was about 2.5 frames late against its picture in 0.9.0 to 0.9.2 (about 42 ms at 59.94, 105 ms at 23.98).
- **No more frame slips on long playback.** With DeckLink output on, file playback follows the card's own clock.
- **Sound locked to the SDI picture, in both audio destinations,** through Play, Pause, seeks, loops and output restarts.
- **Audio-only files play to SDI and HDMI.**

## Fixed

### SDI/HDMI output

- **SDI sound was about 2.5 frames late against its picture** in 0.9.0 to 0.9.2. It is now in sync, to within a sample. Stream calibrations stay valid. If you set a stream's audio offset by ear against an SDI monitor, it will now play about 2.5 frames early: reset it.
- **Files with sound no longer repeat or skip a frame on SDI during long playback.** With DeckLink output on, file playback now follows the card's own clock, as files without sound have since 0.9.2 build 25.
- **SDI sound no longer goes silent for the rest of the session when the Mac's sound output changes during playback,** for example when a screen-sharing app connects or headphones are plugged in.
- **Starting DeckLink output no longer skips a frame on SDI shortly after it starts,** and at 59.94 it no longer sometimes repeats a frame in the first seconds.
- **SDI sound no longer jumps just after Play.** It starts exactly with its picture.

## Improved

### SDI/HDMI output

- **SDI sound stays locked to its picture, sample for sample,** through Play, Pause, seeks, loops and output restarts. Pause ends the sound exactly with the last moving frame.
- **With DeckLink output on and sound on the Mac, the Mac's sound now stays locked to the SDI picture,** through long playback and changes of output device. Before, it followed the window and drifted against SDI over long programs. The window's picture runs ahead of both by about 0.1 s at 59.94 and 0.2 s at 23.98, the time a frame takes to reach SDI.
- **Files whose frame rate differs from the output's** (for example 25p on a 59.94 output) now have continuous SDI sound.
- **44.1 kHz files** are converted once to 48 kHz for SDI.
- **If SDI sound starts silent, Manifold now notices and restarts the output,** a short break in picture and sound. This is rare, and seen when the same card is also capturing (see Known limitations).

## New

### Audio-only files

- **Audio-only files now play to SDI and HDMI,** with the same card the window shows: name, format, channels, sample rate and length. Their sound follows the audio destination like a video file's, so with the destination set to SDI the Mac no longer plays it too. Choose the output mode yourself; Follow source isn't available, as there's no video to follow.

## Known limitations

### SDI/HDMI output

- **Not tested with a genlocked DeckLink card** (house reference). If you use reference and see a problem, let us know.
- **A DeckLink 8K Pro that also captures:** with capture and output on the same sub-device, SDI sound can occasionally start silent. Manifold restarts the output when it does. We recommend a connector mapping in Desktop Video Setup that gives output and capture separate sub-devices, such as "SDI 1 In or Out".
- **2160p above 60 needs dual-link SDI.** In a single-link connector mapping, use a 1080p mode for 100 and 119.88.
- Over HDMI, an HLG picture goes out without its HLG signal, so the display isn't told to switch to HLG. SDI carries HLG.
- **Stills send black to SDI and HDMI.**

### Color

- **Very saturated PQ colors in As macOS Shows It differ slightly from QuickTime.** Colors your display can show match.
- PQ or HLG on primaries other than Rec. 2020 or P3-D65 can't be shown as stated, and those overrides are grayed out.

### Stills

- **DPX stills aren't supported.**
- **Scopes on a gain-map still show the SDR base image,** not the HDR picture on screen.

### Live streams

- **The new SDI timing applies to files.** Live streams to SDI get the 2.5-frame fix but otherwise keep their 0.9.2 timing.
- Interlaced streams (SRT, WHEP, HLS, NDI) still go out over SDI as progressive, with both fields woven into each frame.
- OBS stamps 23.976 fps WHIP video very slightly fast. Through MediaMTX on its default settings, sound and picture drift apart by about a quarter of a second an hour over WHEP. Set `useAbsoluteTimestamp: true` on the MediaMTX path to fix it. Cloudflare Stream corrects it on its own.

## Upgrading from 0.9.2

Nothing moves when you upgrade. Your license, saved streams, shortcuts and settings carry over unchanged.

- **Check any stream offset you set by ear against an SDI monitor.** SDI sound is now about 2.5 frames earlier than before, so such an offset will now play early. Offsets set with Calibrate are unaffected.
- The new SDI timing turns on by itself whenever DeckLink output is on. There's nothing to set.

## For testers: what to look at

- **Long playback to SDI.** Play a long program (30 minutes or more) out to your reference monitor, with sound off the SDI. Watch for any repeated or skipped frame, and listen for sync, especially dialogue at 23.98.
- **Mac speakers with an SDI monitor.** With the audio destination set to Computer, does the sound stay in sync with the SDI picture over a long program?
- **Audio-only files to SDI and HDMI:** WAV, Broadcast WAV, 5.1, MP3 and AAC.
- **Change the Mac's sound output while playing to SDI,** for example by connecting headphones or a screen-sharing app. SDI sound should carry on.
- **If your card also captures,** tell us if SDI sound ever starts silent, and which connector mapping you use.

**Reporting a problem:** Help ▸ Export Diagnostics… saves a report we can read (no license details, and
no file names unless you check the box). Its Picture Recovery section counts any picture or output
recoveries. For a picture problem, Help ▸ Export Frame for Support… saves the frame itself.

**About this tester build:** it carries extra diagnostics. There is a Debug menu, but it stays hidden
unless we ask you to turn it on: one of its items replaces the program audio with a test tone. A few
Control-Option key combinations are test shortcuts and connect on their own: ⌃⌥D and ⌃⌥H connect to your
first saved SRT or WHEP stream, and ⌃⌥N to the first NDI source on your network. Please avoid them.
