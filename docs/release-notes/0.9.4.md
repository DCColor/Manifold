# Manifold 0.9.4

**Version 0.9.4 (build 27) · November 16, 2026 · Beta for testers**

## Summary

0.9.4 adds markers that round-trip with DaVinci Resolve. Press M to mark the frame on screen, even while playing, add a name, notes and one of Resolve's 16 colors, and export a marker EDL that lands frame-exact on a Resolve timeline, drop-frame included. Resolve's markers import back the same way. This release also adds Share Frame through the macOS share menu, clearer names for exported frames, and easier DeckLink setup.

## Highlights

- **Markers.** Press M to mark the frame on screen, even while playing. Name, notes, keywords, duration and Resolve's 16 colors.
- **Round trip with DaVinci Resolve.** Export a marker EDL that Resolve imports frame-exact, drop-frame included, and import Resolve's markers back.
- **Share Frame… (⇧⌘E).** Send the current frame through the macOS share menu: Messages, Mail, AirDrop and more.
- **DeckLink setup.** Open Desktop Video Setup from Manifold, and see which sub-devices your card's connector mapping makes usable.

## New

### Markers

- **Press M to mark the frame on screen,** even while playing. A marker has a name, notes, keywords, a duration and one of Resolve's 16 colors. There is one marker per frame: pressing M on a marked frame opens that marker instead.
- **Keys:** M adds a marker, ⌥M adds one and opens it for editing, ⇧M edits the marker at or before the playhead, I and O mark a span (M then makes a marker covering it), ⇧↑ and ⇧↓ go to the previous and next marker. All are in the new Mark menu and can be changed in Keyboard Shortcuts.
- **The Markers list** sits in a scope slot or in its own window (⌃⌘M), with a thumbnail of each marker's frame. Click a marker to jump to it; double-click to edit it. Filter by color, and delete with ⌫ (with undo).
- **On the timeline,** each marker shows as a flag in its color, and a marker longer than one frame shows as a band. Click a flag to jump to it.
- **View ▸ Show Markers** hides markers without deleting anything.

### Round trip with DaVinci Resolve

- **File ▸ Export Markers…** writes a marker EDL that Resolve imports (Timeline ▸ Import ▸ Timeline Markers from EDL) onto a timeline at the file's own timecode, frame-exact, drop-frame included. Notes keep their line breaks.
- **File ▸ Import Markers…** brings Resolve's markers back (Timeline ▸ Export ▸ Timeline Markers to EDL in Resolve). Importing the same EDL twice adds nothing new, and an import can be undone in one step.
- **Markers also export as CSV and plain text,** for spreadsheets and notes.
- **Files without timecode** count markers from 00:00:00:00. If your Resolve timeline starts at 01:00:00:00 (Resolve's default), set the marker start timecode in the Markers slot's gear menu before exporting, so the markers land on the right frames. When you import an EDL that's an hour off, Manifold offers to shift it.

### Where markers are kept

- **Markers are saved per file on this Mac,** never written into your media, and follow the file if it's renamed or moved. They're never logged or included in Export Diagnostics.
- **Settings ▸ Files ▸ Remember markers between sessions** turns saving off, for example on a shared machine. Delete All Stored Markers… removes everything saved.

### Sharing and export

- **Share Frame… (⇧⌘E)** sends the current frame as a JPEG through the macOS share menu. It's in the File menu, in More (…), and as a Share button next to Export in the full-width control bar.
- **HDR pictures are tone-mapped to SDR for sharing.** For PQ and HLG pictures the menu reads "Share Frame (SDR)…" and the file name ends in " SDR". Export Frame still saves the full-quality PNG, HDR included.
- **Exported and shared frames are named after the clip and its timecode** (for example "MyClip 01-02-14-05"), or the stream and the date and time. Exporting the same frame twice adds " 2" rather than overwriting.
- **Settings ▸ Frame Export** can put exported frames in a folder per clip.

### DeckLink setup

- **Open Desktop Video Setup…** is in Settings ▸ DeckLink Output and the DeckLink menu, when Desktop Video is installed.
- **Sub-devices your card's connector mapping makes inactive are grayed out,** with a note why. If a mapping change makes the output's sub-device unusable while output is on, output stops with a message saying why.

## Known limitations

### Markers

- **Keywords don't travel through EDLs.** Resolve's marker EDL has no field for them; CSV and plain text keep them.
- **Markers are for files.** Live streams, stills and audio-only files don't have markers yet.
- **Thumbnails show the file's own picture,** without a LUT or tag override, and HDR is tone-mapped to SDR. They're for finding your place, not judging the picture.
- **Exports to Premiere, Avid and Final Cut** aren't available yet. They're planned.

### SDI/HDMI output

- **Rarely, one frame can repeat on SDI during playback,** when the window's picture reaches the screen a display refresh late. Manifold recovers at once and the sound follows the picture. We're working on giving the output more margin.
- **Not tested with a genlocked DeckLink card** (house reference).
- **A DeckLink 8K Pro that also captures:** with capture and output on the same sub-device, SDI sound can occasionally start silent. Manifold restarts the output when it does. We recommend a connector mapping that gives output and capture separate sub-devices, such as "SDI 1 In or Out".
- **2160p above 60 needs dual-link SDI.** In a single-link connector mapping, use a 1080p mode for 100 and 119.88.
- Over HDMI, an HLG picture goes out without its HLG signal. SDI carries HLG.
- **Stills send black to SDI and HDMI.**

### Color

- **Very saturated PQ colors in As macOS Shows It differ slightly from QuickTime.** Colors your display can show match.

### Live streams

- Interlaced streams (SRT, WHEP, HLS, NDI) still go out over SDI as progressive, with both fields woven into each frame.
- OBS stamps 23.976 fps WHIP video very slightly fast. Through MediaMTX on its default settings, set `useAbsoluteTimestamp: true` on the path; Cloudflare Stream corrects it on its own.

## Upgrading from 0.9.3

Nothing moves when you upgrade. Your license, saved streams, shortcuts and settings carry over unchanged.

- **The I key is now Mark In.** If you had customized shortcuts that used I, ⇧↑ or ⇧↓, check them in Keyboard Shortcuts.

## For testers: what to look at

- **Markers into Resolve.** Mark a real review with notes and colors, export the EDL, and import it onto your Resolve timeline. Do the markers land on the right frames, with their notes? Try a drop-frame project too.
- **Resolve back into Manifold.** Export markers from a Resolve timeline and import them into Manifold.
- **Marking while playing.** Press M at a specific moment while playing: is the marker on the frame you saw?
- **Files without timecode** against a Resolve timeline starting at 01:00:00:00, using the marker start timecode.
- **Share Frame** to Messages, Mail and AirDrop, with an SDR and an HDR picture.
- **If your card has more than one sub-device,** check the DeckLink menu shows the right ones as usable.

**Reporting a problem:** Help ▸ Export Diagnostics… saves a report we can read (no license details, no
marker text, and no file names unless you check the box). For a picture problem, Help ▸ Export Frame for
Support… saves the frame itself.

**About this tester build:** it carries extra diagnostics. There is a Debug menu, but it stays hidden
unless we ask you to turn it on: one of its items replaces the program audio with a test tone. A few
Control-Option key combinations are test shortcuts and connect on their own: ⌃⌥D and ⌃⌥H connect to your
first saved SRT or WHEP stream, and ⌃⌥N to the first NDI source on your network. Please avoid them.
