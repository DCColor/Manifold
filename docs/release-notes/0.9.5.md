# Manifold 0.9.5

**Version 0.9.5 (build 28) · November 22, 2026 · Beta for testers**

## Summary

0.9.5 makes SDI/HDMI output steadier and brings markers to Premiere Pro. Repeated frames on SDI during playback are now
very rare, including with a scaled display, and SDI sound restarts on its own if it stops.
Manifold now reads the chapter and Premiere markers embedded in your files, exports markers to Premiere Pro and as
chapter lists, and adds a YRGB parade and a Graticule brightness control to the scopes.

## Highlights

- **Steadier SDI/HDMI output.** Repeated frames during playback are now very rare, the picture recovers faster if it
  stalls, and SDI sound restarts on its own if it stops.
- **Markers from your files.** Chapters and Premiere Pro's Comment and Chapter markers show alongside your own, and
  convert to editable markers.
- **Export to Premiere Pro.** Export Markers… writes an XML that Premiere imports as a sequence with your clip and its
  markers.
- **Chapter lists.** Export markers as an ffmpeg chapter file or a YouTube description.
- **YRGB parade and Graticule brightness.** A new four-column scope, and one control for how bright the scope lines are.

## New

### Markers from your files

- **Manifold reads the markers embedded in a file:** QuickTime chapter tracks, MP4 chapters, and Premiere Pro's Comment
  and Chapter markers (from Media Encoder exports with markers included). They show in the Markers list, on the
  timeline and in the Markers window, with thumbnails, beside your own markers.
- **They're the file's, so they can't be edited in Manifold.** Convert them to make editable markers: Convert All File
  Markers to Markers is in the Mark menu and in a file marker's right-click menu. Converting twice adds nothing new.
- **Only converted markers are exported.** The Premiere Pro and chapter list exports say how many file markers they
  left out.
- **A converted marker is one frame long,** unless the file states a duration for it. Premiere's colors carry over;
  chapters with no color become Blue.
- **View ▸ Show File Markers** hides them without changing anything, and the From File filter shows only them.
- **File markers are never written anywhere.** They're read from the file each time it opens, and never logged or
  included in Export Diagnostics.

### Export to Premiere Pro

- **File ▸ Export Markers…**, with the format set to **Premiere Pro (XML)**, writes an XML file. In Premiere,
  File ▸ Import opens it as a sequence holding your clip with its markers, at the file's own timecode (or the start
  timecode you set).
- **Names, notes and durations** carry over, and every marker arrives as a Comment marker. A note's line breaks become
  spaces. Manifold's 16 colors map to the nearest of Premiere's 8.
- **Final Cut Pro and Avid** exports aren't available yet. They're planned for the next build.

### Chapter lists

- **Chapter List — ffmpeg**, a new format in Export Markers…, writes an ffmetadata file that ffmpeg can mux into a file
  as chapters, frame-exact.
- **Chapter List — YouTube** writes a YouTube description, one line per marker. If the list breaks one of YouTube's
  chapter rules (a first chapter at 00:00, at least three chapters, each at least 10 seconds), Manifold says so after the
  export.
- **Both use time from the file's first frame,** not timecode, as YouTube and ffmpeg expect.

### Start timecode

- **Manifold now finds a file's start timecode in more places.** After a timecode track, it reads the timecode stored
  in the container, in QuickTime metadata, or in Premiere's XMP, so more files open at their real timecode.
- **The marker start timecode override** is offered when the file has no timecode track and no timecode in the
  container, so it can also replace a start read from QuickTime metadata or XMP. Where it applies, it's used everywhere:
  the readout, Export Frame names, markers and marker exports.
- **A small icon by the timecode readout** shows when the timecode comes from file metadata or from your override. Hover
  it to see which. The Inspector's Start TC row says where it came from too.

### Markers list

- **Open Markers Editor** in the Markers slot's gear menu opens the Markers window, or brings it to the front.
- **Text size (Small, Medium, Large)** in the gear menu makes the Markers list easier to read in a small slot. It's
  also Marker text size in Settings ▸ Scopes & Guides ▸ Scopes. Medium is the size it was.

### Scopes

- **YRGB Parade:** a new scope with four columns, Y, R, G and B, with its own intensity control. The Waveform and RGB
  Parade are unchanged.
- **Brighter, clearer graticules** on every scope.
- **Settings ▸ Scopes & Guides ▸ Scopes ▸ Graticule brightness** sets how bright the scope lines are. Labels stay
  readable at every setting, and your own lines and targets keep their own brightness.

## Improved

### SDI/HDMI output

- **Repeated frames on SDI during playback are now very rare.** In 0.9.4 a frame could occasionally repeat when the
  window's picture reached the screen a refresh late. Manifold now keeps its timing clear of that edge. In 6.5 hours of
  testing at 23.976, 25 and 59.94, no frame repeated.
- **Scaled displays.** With SDI output at 59.94 and a display running at a scaled resolution, a frame could
  occasionally repeat on SDI. It no longer does.
- **SDI sound restarts if it stops.** If the card stops asking for sound during playback, Manifold restarts the output
  after 3 seconds. If it happens again within a minute, Manifold shows "SDI sound stopped; restart output" instead of
  restarting again. Stop and start output to bring the sound back.
- **SDI sound after a seek.** Fixed: very rarely, after a seek during playback, SDI's sound could stay silent for about a
  minute while the picture played.
- **If the picture stops updating,** Manifold now recovers in about 0.2 seconds instead of about 0.4.

### Markers

- **Hovering a row in the Markers list** now shows its color, timecode and name, the first line of its notes, and its
  keywords. In 0.9.4 it showed nothing.

## Known limitations

### SDI/HDMI output

- **Very rarely, one frame can repeat on SDI at 59.94,** at the start of a Play. We've seen it once in testing.
- **Rarely, the picture can stop updating for a moment.** Manifold recovers within about 0.2 seconds. We're still
  looking for the cause.
- **When you switch files,** the previous file's last frame can show for about a tenth of a second.
- **With output on, the window can drop frames playing 100 or 119.88 fps files on a 120 Hz display.** SDI and HDMI
  aren't affected.
- **Not tested with a genlocked DeckLink card** (house reference).
- **A DeckLink 8K Pro that also captures:** with capture and output on the same sub-device, SDI sound can occasionally
  start silent. Manifold restarts the output when it does. We recommend a connector mapping that gives output and
  capture separate sub-devices, such as "SDI 1 In or Out".
- **2160p above 60 needs dual-link SDI.** In a single-link connector mapping, use a 1080p mode for 100 and 119.88.
- Over HDMI, an HLG picture goes out without its HLG signal. SDI carries HLG.
- **Stills send black to SDI and HDMI.**

### Markers

- **Final Cut Pro and Avid** exports, and reading their markers, are planned for the next build.
- **Only Premiere's Comment and Chapter markers are read.** Its other marker types (such as Segmentation or Web Link)
  are skipped.
- **File markers can't be edited.** Convert them first.
- **EDLs and Premiere XML have no field for keywords.** CSV and plain text keep them, and the EDL and Premiere exports
  can add them to the end of each note.
- **In the Markers list, a marker's notes show on one line.** Hover a row to see its first line and keywords, or
  double-click it to read all the notes.
- **Markers are for files.** Live streams, stills and audio-only files don't have markers yet.
- **Thumbnails show the file's own picture,** without a LUT or tag override, and HDR is tone-mapped to SDR.

### Color

- **Very saturated PQ colors in As macOS Shows It differ slightly from QuickTime.** Colors your display can show match.

### Live streams

- Interlaced streams (SRT, WHEP, HLS, NDI) still go out over SDI as progressive, with both fields woven into each frame.
- OBS stamps 23.976 fps WHIP video very slightly fast. Through MediaMTX on its default settings, set
  `useAbsoluteTimestamp: true` on the path; Cloudflare Stream corrects it on its own.

## Upgrading from 0.9.4

Nothing moves when you upgrade. Your license, saved streams, shortcuts, markers and settings carry over unchanged.

- **Scope graticules are brighter by default.** If you preferred them dimmer, lower Graticule brightness in
  Settings ▸ Scopes & Guides ▸ Scopes.
- **Files that showed 00:00:00:00 may now show their real timecode,** if the timecode is stored somewhere Manifold now
  reads. Markers you made on such a file keep their frames.

## For testers: what to look at

- **Markers into Premiere.** Export a marked review as Premiere Pro XML and import it into Premiere. Do the markers
  land on the right frames, with their names, notes and colors?
- **Premiere's markers into Manifold.** Export from Premiere or Media Encoder with markers included, open the file in
  Manifold, and convert the markers.
- **Chapter lists.** Mux an ffmpeg chapter list into a file, or paste a YouTube list into a video's description.
- **SDI playback** at your usual frame rates, especially 59.94. Tell us about any repeated frame, with the time.
- **A scaled display.** If you run a display at a scaled resolution, play to SDI at 59.94.
- **YRGB Parade and Graticule brightness** on your own material.

**Reporting a problem:** Help ▸ Export Diagnostics… saves a report we can read (no license details, no
marker text, and no file names unless you check the box). For a picture problem, Help ▸ Export Frame for
Support… saves the frame itself.

**About this tester build:** it carries extra diagnostics. There is a Debug menu, but it stays hidden
unless we ask you to turn it on: one of its items replaces the program audio with a test tone. A few
Control-Option key combinations are test shortcuts and connect on their own: ⌃⌥D and ⌃⌥H connect to your
first saved SRT or WHEP stream, and ⌃⌥N to the first NDI source on your network. Please avoid them.
