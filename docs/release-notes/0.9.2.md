# Manifold 0.9.2

**Version 0.9.2 (build 24) · 5 November 2026 · Beta for testers**

## Summary

0.9.2 lets you move your settings between Macs, and opens stills and audio-only files. Export Settings
and Import Settings carry your keyboard shortcuts and saved streams to another Mac, with streams protected
by a password you choose. Manifold now opens still images, shown as macOS shows them and with scopes, and
audio-only files such as WAV, Broadcast WAV, AIFF and MP3. Deleting a saved stream now asks first, and PQ
in As macOS Shows It stays correct when a window moves to another display.

## Highlights

- **Move your settings to another Mac.** Export Settings and Import Settings, in Settings ▸ General, carry your keyboard shortcuts and saved streams, URLs and passphrases included. Streams travel only in a file encrypted with your own password.
- **Importing never changes a stream you already have.** Streams are only ever added, and Undo Import takes an import back.
- **Still images.** PNG, JPEG, TIFF, HDR AVIF, Ultra HDR JPEG and every other format your Mac can read, shown as macOS shows them, with scopes.
- **Audio-only files.** WAV, Broadcast WAV, AIFF, MP3, M4A and AAC open in a player window. Play, seek, loop and meter them.

## New

### Settings transfer

- **Export Settings.** In Settings ▸ General, choose Export Settings…, then tick Keyboard shortcuts, Saved streams or both. The file ends in .manifoldsettings.
- **Saved streams are exported only in an encrypted file.** The file includes each stream's URL and passphrase, so another Mac can connect without retyping them. You choose a password of at least 12 characters, and it can't be recovered: if you forget it, nobody can open the file, Graviton included. A file with shortcuts only is plain text and needs no password.
- **Import Settings.** Choose Import Settings…, or double-click a .manifoldsettings file, or drop one on a player window. Manifold shows what would change before anything does. Nothing changes until you click Import.
- **Importing streams only adds.** A stream with the same name and URL is skipped. The same name with a different URL or passphrase is added as "Name (copy)". Your existing streams are never changed or removed.
- **Shortcuts can merge or replace.** Merge brings in the file's shortcuts and keeps your others. Replace All makes your shortcuts match the file. A shortcut whose key is already in use is shown unticked for you to decide. One whose key macOS reserves isn't imported, and the preview says why.
- **Undo Import.** The summary after an import has an Undo Import button that takes the whole import back. It asks first when it would remove streams.
- **Restore from Backup.** When an import changes your shortcuts, Manifold saves a backup of them first. Settings ▸ General ▸ Restore from Backup… brings one back. The backup covers shortcuts only, not saved streams, so keep your exported file.

### Stills

- **Manifold opens still images:** every SDR format your Mac can read, plus HDR AVIF and Ultra HDR (gain-map) JPEG. A still is shown as macOS shows it, as in Preview. Manifold's color controls don't apply to a still.
- **Scopes on a still** read the values stored in the file. A gain-map file is scoped from its SDR base image, and the scope header says so.
- **The Inspector** shows a still's size, format, color profile, bit depth and HDR details. For an HDR still, the headroom rows say where each value comes from.

### Audio-only files

- **Manifold opens audio-only files:** WAV, AIFF, Broadcast WAV, MP3, M4A, AAC and movies with no picture. The window shows the file's name and format in place of a picture. Play, seek, loop and meter them, with the same channel declarations as a video file's audio.
- **Channel layout by name.** The Inspector names a file's channel layout, such as 5.1 or 7.1, when macOS can read one from the file. This applies to video files too.
- **Broadcast WAV details.** The Inspector shows a Broadcast WAV file's timecode or time reference, and its origination date.
- **Make Manifold the default app for audio files.** Settings ▸ Files now lists WAV, AIFF, MP3, M4A and AAC alongside MOV, MP4 and MXF. Each type is set on its own, and nothing changes until you choose.
- **Audio at launch.** Settings ▸ DeckLink Output ▸ Audio at launch chooses where sound goes when Manifold starts. The toolbar's audio picker now changes it for the session only. With output off, a pick waits for the next time output starts.

## Fixed

### Streams

- **Deleting a saved stream now asks first.** Deleting removes the stream's saved URL and passphrase from this Mac for good, and there was no confirmation.

### Color

- **PQ in As macOS Shows It stays correct when you move a window to another display.** Before, the picture could be wrong on the new display until you changed display mode.
- **The transform readout says "white and black"** instead of "Lb/Lw".

### Files

- **With autoplay off, opening a file over one that is playing no longer leaves the transport stuck,** with Pause showing and nothing moving.

### Interface and shortcuts

- **System Services shortcuts keep their keys inside Manifold.** ⇧⌘L for Search With Google, for example, stopped working while Manifold was in front.

## Known limitations

### Stills and audio-only files

- **SDI and HDMI send black for a still or an audio-only file.** Output keeps its mode and sends black with silent audio while one is open.
- **Audio-only files play on the Mac even when the audio destination is SDI.** Sending them to SDI comes in 0.9.3.
- **DPX stills aren't supported.**
- **Scopes on a gain-map still show the SDR base image,** not the HDR picture on screen.

### Settings transfer

- **The automatic backup covers shortcuts only.** Keep your exported file if you might want your streams back.
- **A forgotten password can't be recovered.** Export again with a new one.

### Color

- **Very saturated PQ colors in As macOS Shows It differ slightly from QuickTime** (as in 0.9.1). Colors your display can show match.
- PQ or HLG on primaries other than Rec. 2020 or P3-D65 can't be shown as stated, and those overrides are greyed out (as in 0.9.1).

### SDI/HDMI output

- **During long playback from a file, SDI output can repeat or skip a single frame every few minutes,** as the output card's clock and your Mac's drift apart (as in 0.9.1).
- **2160p100 and 2160p119.88 output arrive late at the card on every frame** (as in 0.9.1). Use a 1080p mode for these rates.
- Over HDMI, an HLG picture goes out without its HLG signal, so the display isn't told to switch to HLG. SDI carries HLG.
- SDI audio goes silent for up to about a tenth of a second at each seek, scrub release and loop point during file playback.

### Live streams

- Interlaced streams (SRT, WHEP, HLS, NDI) still go out over SDI as progressive, with both fields woven into each frame. An interlaced NDI source arrives already deinterlaced by NDI.
- After a network stall of a second or more, live sound can stay 10–20 ms late for up to a minute.
- OBS stamps 23.976 fps WHIP video very slightly fast. Through MediaMTX on its default settings, sound and picture drift apart by about a quarter of a second an hour over WHEP. Set `useAbsoluteTimestamp: true` on the MediaMTX path to fix it. Cloudflare Stream corrects it on its own.

## Upgrading from 0.9.1

Nothing moves when you upgrade. Your license, saved streams, shortcuts and settings carry over unchanged.

- **Export your settings once you've upgraded.** It's the first way to keep a copy of your saved streams outside Manifold. Use Export Settings…, tick Saved streams, and keep the file and its password somewhere safe.

## For testers: what to look at

- **Export and Import Settings.** Follow the test procedure that came with this build. It uses fake streams and a safety copy of your own settings, and takes about 30 minutes.
- **Moving between two Macs.** If you have two, export your streams on one and import them on the other. They should connect with no URL, passphrase or Keychain prompt.
- **Stills against Preview.** Open the same SDR and HDR stills in Manifold and in Preview. They should look the same.
- **Audio-only files,** including multichannel WAV and Broadcast WAV. Does the Inspector name the layout and show the timecode?
- **Default app for audio.** In Settings ▸ Files, make Manifold the default for one audio type, double-click a file of that type, then set it back.

**Reporting a problem:** Help ▸ Export Diagnostics… saves a report we can read (no license details, and
no file names unless you tick the box). For a picture problem, Help ▸ Export Frame for Support… saves the
frame itself. Never send us a .manifoldsettings file with your own streams in it, or its password.

**About this tester build:** it carries extra diagnostics. There is a Debug menu, but it stays hidden
unless we ask you to turn it on: one of its items replaces the program audio with a test tone. A few
Control-Option key combinations are test shortcuts and connect on their own: ⌃⌥D and ⌃⌥H connect to your
first saved SRT or WHEP stream, and ⌃⌥N to the first NDI source on your network. Please avoid them.
